Delete Your Account In The App

Sign in to Guru Vandan, open Other, select Delete account permanently, and confirm. The app securely verifies your Apple or Google sign-in before deletion. The deletion permanently removes the account, profile, and routine data from Guru Vandan and cannot be undone.

If You Cannot Access The App

Email us from the address used for your Guru Vandan account, or include the relevant account email address in your request.

Data Deleted

  • Your Guru Vandan profile and name information.
  • Your Firebase user record connected with the app.
  • Your saved devotional routine activity and meditation streak data.
  • Your satsang, aarti, meditation, and wisdom interaction records.

Data That May Be Retained

We may retain limited records where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or backup recovery. Such retained data is not used for routine app features and is removed when no longer required.

Processing Time

We aim to complete deletion requests within 7 business days and no later than 30 days unless a longer period is required by law or security obligations.

Before You Request Deletion

Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete your cloud account data. Use the permanent deletion action inside the app or submit a deletion request to remove the account and associated data from Guru Vandan systems.
