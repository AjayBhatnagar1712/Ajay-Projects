Privacy Policy

Guru Vandan is built as a calm devotional companion for satsang, meditation, aarti, and Maharshi Mehi Paramhans wisdom. This policy explains what data is collected and how it is used.

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Information We Collect

Depending on the sign-in method you choose, Guru Vandan may collect your name, email address, phone number, and Firebase user ID. The app also stores your language preference and devotional activity. For signed-in users, this includes app and section visits, satsang and aarti listening duration, meditation duration and completion, routine records, streak counts, and quote views, likes, and shares.

Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, and Firebase services may process account identifiers, device identifiers, approximate location, product interaction and other usage data, diagnostic data, and other technical service data. This information supports sign-in, security, app functionality, service reliability, and analytics.

Support Messages

When you submit the support form, we store your name, reply email, topic, message, optional device and app version, language, consent, and submission time in Firebase. Authorized administrators use these details to handle your request. Messages are not publicly visible. To request removal of a support message, contact us through the support form and include its reference.

How We Use Information

Account Functionality

To keep your profile, name, language choice, and signed-in app experience working across app sessions.

Routine Progress

To show meditation continuity, routine history, satsang completion, and personal progress inside the app.

Wisdom Content

To display Sadguru wisdom quotes in the language selected by the user.

App Maintenance

To show administrators combined usage statistics and per-account activity history, understand which devotional features are most used, diagnose technical problems, and keep the app working.

Sharing And Sale Of Data

Guru Vandan does not sell personal data or share it with data brokers or advertisers. We do not use collected data to track users across other companies' apps or websites. Data is processed by service providers, including Apple, Google Sign-In, and Firebase, for authentication, database storage, app functionality, security, diagnostics, and analytics.

Storage And Security

User data is stored using Firebase Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database. Data is transmitted using secure connections where supported by Firebase and Google services.

No online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we use reasonable safeguards. We do not record meditation audio, device contacts, or content shared outside the app. Support messages are stored only when you submit the support form.

Your Choices

You may sign out of the app at any time. Signing out does not delete your account data. To permanently delete your account and associated data, open Other in the app and select Delete account permanently. You can also use the deletion page below if you cannot access the app.

Children

Guru Vandan is not directed to children. The app is intended for general devotional use by adults and families under their own supervision.
