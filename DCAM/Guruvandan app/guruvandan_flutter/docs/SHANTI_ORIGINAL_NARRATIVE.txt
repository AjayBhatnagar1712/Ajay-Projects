# Shanti: The Rise of Sadguru Maharshi Mehi Paramhans
*Animation narrative, five frames. No on-screen text.*

---

## Background Audio

**Chant (bhajan bed):** a soft, continuous chant under all five frames, rising and falling with each frame's mood:
> *"Bhajo Om Om Prabhu Naam Wahi, Bhajo Om Om Mehi Naam Wahi, Bhajo Om"*

**Spoken voice (deep, calm, unhurried):** one sentence at the peak of each of Frames 1, 3 and 5. While the voice speaks, the chant drops to a whisper.
> *"शान्ति स्थिरता वा निश्चलता को कहते हैं।"*
> *"शांति को जो प्राप्त कर लेते हैं, संत कहलाते हैं।"*
> *"संतों के मत वा धर्म को संतमत कहते हैं।"*

## Frame 1: The Restless Seeker
**1885 – 1904**

Dawn over a village in Bihar. Mist lies on the fields and a thin wisp of smoke rises from a mud hut. The camera drifts down to a small boy sitting alone under a tree. He presses an iron nail into the earth, pours water over it from a small brass lota, folds his hands and closes his eyes. Wind moves through the leaves around him, but he does not move.

The leaves swirl and dissolve into scattered exam papers, and time moves forward. A crowded examination hall in 1904, full of scratching pens, a ticking clock, and restless young faces. The boy is now a young man. He writes a few lines, pauses, and puts down his pen. He stands, bows slightly to the invigilator, and walks toward the open door. Bright light floods in through it. He steps into the light without looking back.

**Audio:** The chant begins faintly, almost from a distance, carried on the wind. As he steps through the door, it softens and the voice speaks:
> *"शान्ति स्थिरता वा निश्चलता को कहते हैं।"*

---

## Frame 2: The Guru Opens the Inner Door
**1909 – 1919**

A long, dusty road at dusk. The young seeker walks it alone, and the seasons change around him: rain, heat, falling leaves. His search is long.

He reaches a quiet room lit by a single oil lamp. An elderly sage, Baba Devi Sahab, sits in stillness. The seeker bows and touches his feet. The Guru places his hand gently on the young man's head. The flame of the lamp becomes steady and upright.

The camera moves slowly toward the seeker's closed eyes, and further, into the space between the brows. Inside there is deep indigo darkness. First a faint glow of mantra, like a soft pulse. Then the gentle outline of the Guru's form. Then a single, brilliant point of light appears at the centre and holds perfectly still.

**Audio:** The chant grows warmer and fuller as the Guru's hand touches his head. A single temple bell rings when the point of light appears.

---

## Frame 3: Into the Cave: Darkness, Light, Sound
**1933 – 1934 · Kuppaghat**

Night on the Ganga. Moonlight shimmers on the water. Along the riverbank, a narrow cave mouth is half hidden by vines. The seeker, now a mature ascetic, enters and sits. The seasons pass outside the cave while he stays completely still inside it.

The animation turns inward. There is total darkness, then scattered sparks like stars, then a steady, spreading radiance that fills the whole frame with golden-white light. Inside the light, faint ripples of sound appear as circles expanding outward. The soul rises through them, layer after layer, toward a single luminous stillness beyond all light and sound.

Outside, the first dawn light touches the cave mouth. He steps out. His face is calm and radiant. He has become a Sant.

**Audio:** The chant pulls back to near-silence. A soft, pure inner resonance, like a distant conch or bell, rises and swells as the light spreads. At the moment of stillness, everything falls silent, and the voice speaks:
> *"शांति को जो प्राप्त कर लेते हैं, संत कहलाते हैं।"*

The chant then returns, now bright and full.

---

## Frame 4: The Science of the Spirit
**1934 onward**

A simple ashram room. The Sant sits at a low wooden desk with an ink pot and pen and writes steadily. Pages fill and turn.

The ink lifts off the pages and forms a glowing map in the air: the body at the base; darkness above it; then light; then sound; and at the top, the soundless supreme. A small point of light, the soul, travels upward along the map, stage by stage. The map spreads further, and the scriptures of different traditions appear as streams of light flowing into the same single path.

The bound books settle onto a shelf: *Satsang Yog*, *Mokshadarshan*. Their spines glow softly.

**Audio:** The chant continues at a steady, meditative pace with a gentle tanpura drone underneath. There is no spoken voice in this frame.

---

## Frame 5: A River of Seekers
**1934 – 1986, and today**

A village path. The Sant walks it alone. A farmer puts down his plough and follows. Then a mother with a child, then an old man with a stick. The line grows to ten, then a hundred. The camera rises into a wide aerial shot of a vast satsang: lakhs of people sitting in silent meditation, spreading out like a sea of lamps.

The seasons pass once more. His samadhi at Kuppaghat at dawn, with marigold petals drifting across its steps.

The scene changes gently to the present day. A seeker sits cross-legged in a quiet modern room, holding a phone with the Guruvandan app glowing softly. He puts the phone down, closes his eyes, and sits in the same stillness. The final image matches the first: a single steady flame.

**Audio:** The chant swells to its fullest, joined by many voices like a satsang choir. It then fades to a hush, and the voice speaks:
> *"संतों के मत वा धर्म को संतमत कहते हैं।"*

---

## Closing
The screen goes black except for the single lamp. The full line is spoken once, unbroken:
> *"शान्ति स्थिरता वा निश्चलता को कहते हैं। शांति को जो प्राप्त कर लेते हैं, संत कहलाते हैं। संतों के मत वा धर्म को संतमत कहते हैं।"*

The chant returns softly for one last cycle, *"Bhajo Om Om Prabhu Naam Wahi, Bhajo Om Om Mehi Naam Wahi, Bhajo Om,"* and fades as the flame stays lit.
