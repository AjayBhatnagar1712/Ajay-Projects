Smoothness is a production step.

  • These are reference images, not completed videos.
  • Use gentle motion, real foot contact, consistent hair and robes. No fast cuts or face reveals.
  • For 05 → 06 and 11 → 12, a clean last frame from the preceding approved clip gives better continuity than the supplied fallback.
