## Other art

The kit reuses five earlier built-in image-generation references (child, blessing, seated cave, village and lamp), with three repeated files for clear scene numbering. Eleven new reference images were generated or edited with the built-in image tool for this kit, including the samadhi adaptation and optional mountain. The optional mountain received one targeted extra correction to remove a duplicate staff. No video generation was submitted and no Runway video credits were spent.

Character/style references are the user-supplied monk artwork and existing project-generated art. These are cinematic story interpretations, not archival images. Their exact new still-image prompts are preserved in `IMAGE_PROMPTS.md`; complete video prompts are in `shots.json` and `prompts/`.
