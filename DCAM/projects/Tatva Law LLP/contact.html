Before You Reach Out

Keep the first message brief, factual and well-documented.

A concise first summary makes it easier to understand urgency, identify the relevant procedure and prepare for a useful consultation.

01

Share the issue type

Mention whether the matter concerns fraud, a frozen account, a cyber complaint, harassment or unauthorized access.

02

Add key dates and amounts

Include transaction date, amount, account status and any deadlines or urgent developments.

03

Include reference numbers

If available, share complaint IDs, FIR details, bank ticket numbers or platform case references.

04

Keep documents ready

Have screenshots, notices, communication records and payment details ready for structured review.
