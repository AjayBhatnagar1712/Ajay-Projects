## Audience

School principal and academic leadership.

## Objective

Request permission to conduct a student seminar and explain clearly, in simple language, what DCAM Classes offers students.

## Narrative

1. Present the seminar request.
2. Explain why the seminar helps students.
3. Show the full DCAM student offering in one clear overview.
4. Break the platform into learning, practice, and support.
5. Show that the seminar is easy for the school to host.
6. Close with the value to the school and the approval request.

## Visual Direction

- Use the DCAM website palette: dark navy backgrounds, cyan/sky/blue highlights, white text.
- Keep the layout cleaner and less busy than the previous version.
- Use the DCAM logo on the cover and in slide branding.

## Editability

- All visible text is editable PowerPoint text.
- Layout panels are native PowerPoint shapes.
- The logo is embedded as an image asset.
