## Audience

Students from classes 9 to 12 attending the seminar.

## Objective

Explain DCAM Classes in simple student language and show the step-by-step procedure for using the platform after the seminar.

## Narrative Arc

1. Welcome students and explain the purpose of the seminar.
2. Introduce DCAM Classes in easy language.
3. Show the full set of student tools.
4. Explain how to start: account, dashboard, and basic setup.
5. Explain how to choose the right path: learning, batches, practice.
6. Show how to learn, practise, ask doubts, and stay updated.
7. Explain how students from classes 9 to 10 and 11 to 12 can use the platform differently.
8. Close with a simple weekly routine.

## Visual Direction

- Match the DCAM website palette: navy, cyan, sky blue, and white.
- Keep the deck simple, clean, and student-friendly.
- Use the DCAM logo for consistent branding.

## Editability

- All visible text is native editable PowerPoint text.
- Panels and layout elements are native PowerPoint shapes.
- The logo is embedded as an image asset.
